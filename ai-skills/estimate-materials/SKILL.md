name: estimate-materials
description: Build a complete renovation shopping bill with quantities, tools and consumables, estimated cost allowances and Excel export. Research individual products only on explicit request.
---
Establish location/currency, chosen scope/design, budget, finish and items to retain. Reuse chat answers and photos. For a requested BOM, provide a complete useful first bill with clearly labelled assumptions, rather than repeatedly asking permission or creating a tiny sample. Use create_material_estimate; do not run internet search. The app displays quantities, estimated prices and totals directly in chat and saves the same bill in Materials with Excel export.

Audit the whole job before saving: protection; cleaning/degreasing; repair and substrate preparation; compatible primer; chosen paint/coating/floor/furniture; correct application tools; fixings and adhesives; finishing; clean-up; PPE appropriate to the products. Painting commonly needs separate rows for paint, primer if needed, cleaner, filler if needed, abrasive, masking tape, protective sheet, compatible roller sleeve, roller frame, paint tray, cutting-in brush, and gloves. Add a pole or step stool only when access needs it. For drilled shelves or handles include a drill/driver if not owned, suitable bits, substrate-compatible plugs and screws, level/tape measure and a cable/pipe detector when needed. Flooring needs its own substrate/underlay/adhesive/cutting/threshold supplies as appropriate. Do not automatically require drilling or demolition for a paint-only job.

Make each row a specific purchasable product, not a bundled 'paint tools set', 'hardware allowance' or 'storage and laundry organisation'. Split a brush, roller, tray, hamper, shelf, handles and fasteners into their own rows. Allow up to 40 rows when scope merits it; do not pad a simple job with unnecessary purchases. Include reusable tools once, state reuse/borrow/rent options and omit tools the user confirms they own. Use specification to capture purpose, compatibility, size, finish/colour and important constraints without requiring an imaginary exact pack size. Only the opt-in product comparison searches retailers and recalculates a chosen pack.

Use appropriate purchase units (piece, pot, pack, bottle, roll, bag, litre, m²), area/perimeter/manual basis, coats, waste and coverage. For tools use manual quantity, coats=1, waste=0, coverage=null. When CAD is absent use clearly provisional manual quantities and assumptions; never create a 2D drawing to unlock calculations. Distinguish purchased paint litres from the area they cover. Avoid double-counting waste or multiplying tools by coats. Labour, delivery and professional work belong in exclusions rather than fake product prices.

Quantity assessment: use measurements to record net floorArea, wallArea, ceilingArea and perimeter independently of CAD, each nullable when irrelevant. Estimate from visible photos with an explicit scale reference and uncertainty, confirmed=false; reuse dimensions the user supplies. For rectangular rooms use length × width for floor/ceiling, 2 × (length + width) × height minus known door/window and retained tiled areas for walls. For a partially painted room include only the treated surfaces, not the whole room. State deductions and dimensions in measurement notes. For sloped ceilings, alcoves and irregular shapes break surfaces into simple portions; do not force a rectangular assumption. A photo cannot give precise hidden surface areas. Ask only for the most useful missing dimension if needed, otherwise give a useful provisional bill. The user can edit measured surfaces directly in the project.

Use coverage as area per ONE coat per original purchase unit; coats multiply once and waste applies once. Do not convert area to litres manually and then also apply coverage. Use manualQuantity as the basis fallback, not a precomputed coated/waste quantity. Separate paint/primer coverage assumptions; tile coating, cabinetry and wall paint need their own treated areas. Count doors/handles/fittings explicitly; tools are whole pieces, once each. An owned tool is omitted or a documented zero-cost reuse, never multiplied by surface area. Make quantities and allowances consistent with the actual proposed visual and work steps.

Only real retrieved product-page URLs and evidenced prices may be presented as sourced shopping products. Never fabricate links, stock, pack size, drying times or prices. Unmatched rows remain honest allowances. Do not describe a partial match as a fully verified basket. No search runs on a new bill, page load or export. Explain that allowances can later be replaced by optional comparisons of specific products. Keep the final reply polished, with a short recommendation and any essential client choices; no planning narration, search debugging, raw JSON or duplicate Markdown table. For a construction plan, use the saved BOM ID and exact row indexes, so the app can attach the same products to each work step.
