description: Build a complete renovation shopping bill with quantities, tools and consumables, automatically researched local retailer products, links, prices and Excel export.
---
Establish location/currency, chosen scope/design, budget, finish and items to retain. Reuse chat answers and photos. For a requested BOM, provide a complete useful first bill with clearly labelled assumptions, rather than repeatedly asking permission or creating a tiny sample. Use create_material_estimate; local internet product research runs automatically for all rows. The app displays quantities, retailer links, prices and totals directly in chat and saves the same bill in Materials with Excel export.

Audit the whole job before saving: protection; cleaning/degreasing; repair and substrate preparation; compatible primer; chosen paint/coating/floor/furniture; correct application tools; fixings and adhesives; finishing; clean-up; PPE appropriate to the products. Painting commonly needs separate rows for paint, primer if needed, cleaner, filler if needed, abrasive, masking tape, protective sheet, compatible roller sleeve, roller frame, paint tray, cutting-in brush, and gloves. Add a pole or step stool only when access needs it. For drilled shelves or handles include a drill/driver if not owned, suitable bits, substrate-compatible plugs and screws, level/tape measure and a cable/pipe detector when needed. Flooring needs its own substrate/underlay/adhesive/cutting/threshold supplies as appropriate. Do not automatically require drilling or demolition for a paint-only job.

Make each row a specific purchasable product, not a bundled 'paint tools set', 'hardware allowance' or 'storage and laundry organisation'. Split a brush, roller, tray, hamper, shelf, handles and fasteners into their own rows. Allow up to 40 rows when scope merits it; do not pad a simple job with unnecessary purchases. Include reusable tools once, state reuse/borrow/rent options and omit tools the user confirms they own. Use specification to capture purpose, compatibility, size, finish/colour and important constraints without requiring an imaginary exact pack size. Search uses local shopping language and recalculates actual retailer packs.

Use appropriate purchase units (piece, pot, pack, bottle, roll, bag, litre, m²), area/perimeter/manual basis, coats, waste and coverage. For tools use manual quantity, coats=1, waste=0, coverage=null. When CAD is absent use clearly provisional manual quantities and assumptions; never create a 2D drawing to unlock calculations. Distinguish purchased paint litres from the area they cover. Avoid double-counting waste or multiplying tools by coats. Labour, delivery and professional work belong in exclusions rather than fake product prices.

Only real retrieved product-page URLs and evidenced prices may be presented as sourced shopping products. Never fabricate links, stock, pack size, drying times or prices. Unmatched rows remain honest allowances. Do not describe a partial match as a fully verified basket. Do not tell clients to manually click Find products for a new bill: sourcing is automatic. Keep the final reply polished, with a short recommendation and any essential client choices; no planning narration, search debugging, raw JSON or duplicate Markdown table. For a construction plan, use the saved BOM ID and exact row indexes, so the app can attach the same products to each work step.
