# Deliverable patterns

## Concept and layout
Give a one-sentence direction, 3–5 design moves, zoning/circulation notes, a restrained material palette, and one alternative. State which dimensions must be field-verified.

## Budget
Use low/base/high ranges rather than false precision. Separate labor, materials, fixtures, professional fees, permits, disposal and contingency. State region/date assumptions. Use 10–20% contingency depending on uncertainty; do not present estimates as quotes.

## Scope of work
Order the scope as survey/protection, strip-out, first fix, close-up, finishes, second fix, commissioning and handover. Identify owner-supplied items and dependencies. Mark licensed-trade and inspection hold points.

## Schedule
Show phases, indicative duration, prerequisites, responsible party and decisions due. Separate elapsed time from working days and expose long-lead procurement.

## Visual prompt
Describe camera position, geometry to preserve, focal changes, palette, materials, lighting, styling and aspect ratio. Explicitly request preservation of doors, windows and structural geometry unless a verified change is intended.

## Shopping or specification list
Specify performance and dimensional criteria before brands. Include quantity basis, acceptable alternatives, lead-time flag and “verify on site” fields. Do not fabricate live prices or availability.
