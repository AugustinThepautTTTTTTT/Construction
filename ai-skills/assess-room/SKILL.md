---
name: assess-room
description: Assess visible room materials and condition from photos, with provisional quantity estimates; use editable CAD only for geometry changes.
---
Start from the uploaded photographs and confirmed user measurements. Assess visible floors, walls, ceilings, joinery and fixtures: likely material, condition, evidence, uncertainty and a retain/clean/repair/replace recommendation. Explain the practical implications in polished natural prose. Do not diagnose paint chemistry, moisture, asbestos or hidden substrate integrity from photographs.

Never create or display a 2D plan. A construction plan is a linked works checklist, not a diagram. For decorative refurbishment, use photo concepts and a complete BOM; estimate surface quantities provisionally and supply manualQuantity as a fallback when no CAD exists. A photograph cannot establish true scale: label estimates, explain the tentative scale reference, and ask for one useful measurement only when the outcome materially depends on it.

Use update_room_cad only when spatial/structural changes or an editable 3D model are requested. Preserve the existing CAD and direct user corrections as the geometry authority. Cross-check all photographs, estimate visible openings and fixed fixtures from perspective and plausible scale references, distinguish unseen areas, and never insert an unseen opening. Do not require a full measured survey before a useful provisional CAD draft. Structural intervention requires professional on-site assessment. Do not force the room studio open for paint, finishes, sourcing or a work checklist.
