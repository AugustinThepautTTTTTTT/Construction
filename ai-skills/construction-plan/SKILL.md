---
name: construction-plan
description: Create or revise a practical sequenced renovation works plan linked to a complete saved bill of materials, including preparation, tools, drying periods and completion checks.
---
Treat a construction plan, work plan, chantier, programme de travaux or renovation schedule as an ordered work checklist, never a floor drawing. Use create_construction_plan. Keep CAD exclusively for geometry changes. The app renders the plan in chat and in the project's Work plan section.

Use the latest chosen design, visible condition assessment, constraints and complete BOM. If no bill exists or it omits required preparation, tools or fixings, first save a complete bill with create_material_estimate, then use its returned id as estimateId. Preserve the exact row indexes. Never make up a bill ID or silently switch to a bill from another room. Link every needed material, consumable and tool through materialIndexes. Product titles, quantities, current prices and retailer links are resolved by the app from the linked bill; do not copy or invent them in the step text. Include own/reuse/borrow decisions in assumptions and avoid charging duplicate tools. When a bill is revised, issue an updated linked plan rather than pretending its old references changed.

Order the practical work: inspect and measure; clear/protect; clean/degrease; repair/prepare; prime if required; apply chosen finish with the correct tools; respect product-label drying and curing; fit hardware/storage; clean up; inspect the result. Tailor this to the real scope: no irrelevant demolition or generic painting sequence for furniture assembly. State short actionable instructions, appropriate tool technique, surface compatibility and what makes each step complete. Include prior step indexes (zero-based) in dependsOn; these must always be earlier steps. Distinguish active work duration from waiting/drying time. Use manufacturer instructions for exact recoat, ventilation, adhesive and curing requirements; give provisional ranges only when unknown, explicitly marked. Do not describe unknown drying times as verified.

Set professionalRequired for structural, electrical, gas, waterproofing or regulated interventions requiring local expertise. Explain the assessment/coordination step, not speculative demolition or hazardous DIY. Include concealed cable/pipe checks before drilling, appropriate fixings for the actual substrate, and protection/ventilation relevant to the chosen products. Give sensible checks such as adhesion, level/alignment, even coverage, clear door swings, watertightness where professionally applicable and snagging. Keep uncertainty explicit without an unnecessary disclaimer wall.

Deliver only a polished short introduction and key client decisions. Do not narrate your planning, tools, validation, search attempts or internal reasoning. Do not repeat the rendered checklist as a Markdown table. Explain genuinely missing decisions briefly rather than inventing construction details.

Default to an implementation plan with estimated material allowances; never run product searches as a side effect. Selected retailer products and corrected quantities automatically flow from the linked bill into step materials. In the project workspace clients can open each step, jump to the exact material to compare/select products, and save completion progress. Keep material references consistent so that consultation and shopping do not require returning to the chat.
